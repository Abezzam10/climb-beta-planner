Why I built this

I like finding the right mix of fun, physics, math and computer science in a sport. I enjoy my work, and I recharge by doing sports. Climbing and bouldering are two of them.

Climbing is very personal. What feels easy to you can feel hard to someone else. Your height, arm length and body shape all change it. If you climb, picture a cramped V2 when you are 6'2.

So I wanted a tool that thinks about the climber, not only the wall. This is an early, small step toward that.

About this project

An early test that turns a wall photo and a few holds you mark into a suggested order of hand and foot moves, using simple reach and balance rules. It does not say a route is best, safe, or possible. Planned later: automatic hold finding, and offline tests to improve the search on walls it was not tuned on.

  • It works in flat 2D. It cannot see wall angle, depth, hold shape, chalk or grip.
  • Reach is a rough guess from your size: arm about 39% of arm span, leg about 50% of height.
  • "Balance" is only how far the hips drift sideways from the feet. It is not a real force calculation.
  • Hold types come from you (or the sample wall). Nothing is found automatically yet.
  • It does not know your strength or grade.
